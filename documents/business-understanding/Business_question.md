# Business Questions

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the key Business Questions that the Modern Data Platform aims to answer. These questions serve as the foundation for developing Business Requirements, Data Requirements, the Metric Dictionary, and Executive Dashboards throughout the project.

---

## 2. Business Questions

| ID | Business Question | Objective | Recommendation | Priority | Owner |
|----|-------------------|-----------|----------------|----------|-------|
| **BQ1** | Which customer segments should be prioritized for marketing and customer engagement strategies based on purchasing behavior? | Identify customer segments based on purchasing behavior to support targeted marketing and customer engagement strategies. | Prioritize marketing campaigns and customer engagement programs for each customer segment according to its business value. | High | Marketing Team |
| **BQ2** | Which customer segments generate the highest business value in each state and should be prioritized for customer retention strategies? | Identify high-value customer segments across different states to optimize customer retention strategies. | Prioritize customer retention initiatives in states where high-value customer segments contribute the most to business performance. | High | Marketing Team |
| **BQ3** | Which product categories contribute the most to sales performance in each state and should be prioritized in business strategies? | Identify the product categories that contribute the most to sales performance across different states to support product and sales optimization. | Prioritize investment and promotional strategies for high-performing product categories in each state. | High | Sales Team |
| **BQ4** | Which sellers should be prioritized for performance improvement in each state to enhance customer experience and business performance? | Identify underperforming sellers based on business and customer experience metrics. | Prioritize performance improvement programs for sellers with the greatest impact on customer satisfaction and business performance. | High | Sales Team |
