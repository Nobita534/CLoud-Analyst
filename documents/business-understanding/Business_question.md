# Business Questions

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the key Business Questions that the Modern Data Platform aims to answer. These questions serve as the foundation for developing Business Requirements, Data Requirements, the Metric Dictionary, and Executive Dashboards throughout the project.

---

## 2. Business Questions

| ID | Business Question | Objective | Recommendation | Priority | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **BQ1** | **Which customer segments should be prioritized for marketing and customer engagement based on purchasing behavior and business value?** | Identify customer segments based on **Recency, Frequency, and Monetary (RFM)** metrics to understand customer purchasing behavior and business value. | Prioritize retention, re-engagement, and promotional strategies according to the value and purchasing behavior of each customer segment. | High | Marketing Team |
| **BQ2** | **Which customer segments generate the highest business value across different geographic areas?** | Identify high-value customer segments and compare their business contribution across customer states and cities. | Prioritize customer retention and localized marketing strategies in geographic areas with a high concentration of valuable customer segments. | High | Marketing Team |
| **BQ3** | **Which product categories and products contribute the most to sales performance across different geographic areas?** | Identify high-performing product categories and products based on sales revenue and order activity across states and cities. | Prioritize product promotion, assortment, and sales strategies for high-performing products and categories in each geographic market. | High | Sales Team |
| **BQ4** | **Which sellers have the greatest impact on sales performance and customer satisfaction, and which sellers require performance improvement?** | Evaluate seller performance using sales, order activity, and customer review scores. | Prioritize seller development and performance improvement for sellers with significant business contribution but comparatively poor customer satisfaction. | High | Sales Team |
| **BQ5** | **How does sales performance change across different time periods, and which periods contribute most to business revenue?** | Analyze revenue, order volume, and customer purchasing activity across different time periods. | Identify high- and low-performing periods to support sales planning, promotional campaigns, and resource allocation. | High | Sales Team |
| **BQ6** | **Which payment methods are most commonly used by customers, and how do payment behaviors vary across different customer segments?** | Analyze payment methods, payment value, and installment behavior across customer segments and geographic areas. | Optimize payment options and promotional strategies based on customer payment preferences and purchasing behavior. | Medium | Sales Team |
| **BQ7** | **Which geographic areas have the highest sales activity and customer concentration?** | Identify geographic areas with high customer concentration, order volume, and revenue contribution. | Prioritize localized marketing, seller development, and business expansion strategies in high-potential geographic markets. | High | Sales Team |
| **BQ8** | **How does customer satisfaction vary across product categories and sellers?** | Analyze review scores to identify products, categories, and sellers associated with high or low customer satisfaction. | Prioritize product and seller improvement initiatives where low customer satisfaction has a significant impact on business performance. | High | Sales Team |
