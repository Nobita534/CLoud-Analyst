# Business Problem

## 1. Business Context

Olist is a Brazilian e-commerce marketplace that connects sellers with customers through multiple online sales channels. During its daily operations, the platform continuously generates data related to customers, orders, products, payments, reviews, and deliveries.

As the volume of business data continues to grow, the company has an opportunity to leverage data analytics for better business decision-making. To fully utilize this data, a modern data platform is required to integrate, standardize, and transform raw data into valuable business insights.

---

## 2. Current Situation

Currently, Olist stores business data across multiple relational tables, representing different aspects of the business process, including customers, orders, products, payments, and deliveries.

Although the data is comprehensive, business analysis mainly relies on individual SQL queries or manual reporting. This makes it difficult to consolidate information, monitor business performance consistently, and answer business questions efficiently.

---

## 3. Business Problem

Although Olist collects comprehensive business data, stakeholders still face several challenges when transforming data into actionable insights.

The main business problems include:

- It is difficult to identify high-value customers for customer retention and marketing strategies.
- It is difficult to analyze customer purchasing behavior and consumption patterns from different business perspectives.
- It is difficult to monitor sales performance across different time periods, product categories, and geographic locations.
- It is difficult to evaluate overall business performance using consistent and reliable analytical data.
- Report generation and data consolidation still require significant manual effort, making the analysis process time-consuming and difficult to scale.

As a result, stakeholders cannot efficiently answer critical business questions, leading to slower and less data-driven decision-making.

---

## 4. Business Objective

This project aims to build a **Modern Data Platform** by integrating Cloud ETL, Analytics Engineering, and Business Intelligence to:

- Standardize the data ingestion and transformation process.
- Build a scalable Data Warehouse for business analytics.
- Establish a Single Source of Truth for business metrics.
- Deliver interactive dashboards that support business analysis and decision-making.

---

## 5. Stakeholders (Assumption)

The primary stakeholders of this project are assumed to include:

- Executive Management
- Sales Team
- Marketing Team
- Business Analysts
- Data Analysts

---

## 6. Why Analytics is Needed

A centralized analytics platform enables the organization to transform raw business data into standardized metrics, dashboards, and actionable insights.

By combining Cloud ETL, Data Warehouse, Analytics Engineering, and Power BI, the platform provides a reliable foundation for answering business questions, monitoring business performance, and supporting data-driven decision-making.

---

## 7. Summary

This project addresses the challenges of business data analysis by building a Modern Data Platform that supports Business Intelligence. The platform establishes a standardized analytics foundation for defining business requirements, designing the Data Warehouse, implementing business metrics, and developing executive dashboards in the subsequent phases.
