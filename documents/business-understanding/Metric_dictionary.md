# Metric Dictionary

## 1. Overview

### 1.1. Purpose

This document defines the business metrics and Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) used throughout the analytics platform.

It provides standardized metric definitions, calculation logic, required fields, and ownership to ensure consistency across Analytics Engineering, Business Intelligence dashboards, and business reports.

---

## 2. Metric Dictionary

| Metric ID | Metric Name | Metric Type | Business Requirement | Definition | Formula | Required Fields | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **KPI-01** | Total Revenue | KPI | BR3, BR4, BR5, BR6, BR7, BR8, BR9, BR10, BR13, BR15 | Total payment value generated from successfully completed orders within the selected analysis period. | `SUM(payment_value)` for completed orders | `order_id`, `order_status`, `payment_value` | Sales Team |
| **KPI-02** | Purchase Frequency | Customer Metric | BR1, BR2, BR3, BR4, BR9, BR10 | Number of distinct completed orders placed by a customer within the 12-month period preceding each snapshot date. | `COUNT(DISTINCT order_id)` grouped by `customer_unique_id`, `snapshot_date` | `customer_unique_id`, `order_id`, `order_purchase_timestamp`, `snapshot_date` | Marketing Team |
| **KPI-03** | Recency | Customer Metric | BR1, BR2, BR3, BR4 | Number of days between each snapshot date and the customer's most recent completed purchase within the preceding 12-month period. | `Snapshot Date - MAX(order_purchase_timestamp)` grouped by `customer_unique_id`, `snapshot_date` | `customer_unique_id`, `order_purchase_timestamp`, `snapshot_date` | Marketing Team |
| **KPI-04** | Customer Count | Basic Metric | BR2, BR3, BR4, BR13 | Number of unique customers with at least one completed order within the selected analysis period. | `COUNT(DISTINCT customer_unique_id)` | `customer_unique_id`, `order_id`, `order_status` | Marketing Team |
| **KPI-05** | Voucher Usage Rate | Ratio | BR11, BR12 | Percentage of completed orders that used at least one voucher payment. | `Voucher Orders / Completed Orders × 100` | `order_id`, `order_status`, `payment_type` | Marketing Team |
| **KPI-06** | Repeat Purchase Rate | KPI | BR1, BR3, BR4 | Percentage of customers who placed two or more distinct completed orders within the selected analysis period. | `COUNT(Customer with ≥ 2 Orders) / COUNT(DISTINCT customer_unique_id) × 100` | `customer_unique_id`, `order_id`, `order_status`, `order_purchase_timestamp` | Marketing Team |
| **KPI-07** | Total Orders | Basic Metric | BR3, BR4, BR5, BR6, BR7, BR9, BR10, BR13 | Number of distinct successfully completed orders within the selected analysis period. | `COUNT(DISTINCT order_id)` | `order_id`, `order_status` | Sales Team |
| **KPI-08** | Average Review Score | KPI | BR7, BR8, BR14, BR15 | Average customer review score associated with successfully completed orders within the selected analysis period. | `AVG(review_score)` | `order_id`, `order_status`, `review_score` | Sales Team |
| **KPI-09** | Monetary | Customer Metric | BR1, BR2, BR3, BR4 | Total payment value generated by a customer from successfully completed orders within the 12-month period preceding each snapshot date. | `SUM(payment_value)` grouped by `customer_unique_id`, `snapshot_date` | `customer_unique_id`, `order_id`, `payment_value`, `order_purchase_timestamp`, `snapshot_date` | Marketing Team |
